- `FIX` ⌃ — Fine adjustments, and secondary click. → ⌃ (Control): Control-click a selected region to open its shortcut menu.
- `FIX` ↩ — Sends the playhead to the start of the project. → ↩ (Return): Sends the playhead to the start of the project.
- `FIX` ⌫ — Removes the selection. → ⌫ (Delete): Deletes the selected regions.
- `FIX` ␣ — Play / Stop. The most used key in the program. → ␣ (Space): Play / Stop.
- `FIX` callout — Hold ⌥ while dragging a region and you copy it instead of moving it. ⌥-click a channel's Volume fader and it returns to 0 dB. ⌥-click a Solo button and it becomes an exclusive solo — that channel alone.
